Curious Buddy: safety and privacy

A learning app for children aged 8 to 14, built to be safe in class and at home.

✔ Works offline. Every game, story and answer is inside the app. No internet needed.
✔ No accounts. No sign-up, no logins, no passwords.
✔ No ads. No adverts. Children can never buy anything: the one-time unlock is only in the Grown-ups area.
✔ No tracking. No analytics, advertising IDs or location.
✔ No chat. Children can't contact anyone, and no one can contact them.
✔ No personal details. The app never asks for a name, age, school, address or photo.

What the app stores, and where

  • Only progress (missions and stories finished, quiz stars, game levels, Buddy's outfit), a few settings, and the child's own drawings.
  • It stays on the device, in storage private to the app. It is never sent anywhere, and Android cloud backup is turned off.
  • Conversations with Buddy are never saved. They disappear when the app closes.
  • A grown-up can see everything that is stored, and delete it, in the Grown-ups area. Uninstalling the app deletes it all.

Talking with Buddy

  • Buddy is not an AI chatbot. Every answer is written in advance and reviewed, so a child can't get an unexpected or unsuitable reply.
  • Buddy says honestly that it is a computer character, with no real feelings, and that it can make mistakes.
  • A safety check runs before every answer. Questions about personal details, violence, drugs, adult topics, medicines or rule-breaking get a gentle reply that points the child to a trusted grown-up.
  • If a child says they are being hurt, don't feel safe, or are sad or worried, Buddy is kind, says it is not their fault, and tells them to talk to a teacher, school nurse, carer or family member. Buddy never promises to keep a secret.
  • When a child shares personal details by accident, Buddy doesn't show or repeat them.

The microphone

  • Talking is optional. Typing works without it.
  • The microphone is only used when the child taps it, for up to 15 seconds, and only after the app asks first. It never listens in the background.
  • By default, speech is recognised on the device itself. If a phone can't do that, Buddy says so and doesn't listen.
  • A grown-up can choose to allow the phone's online speech service. It is off unless they turn it on. If they do, that service (often Google) may receive the audio under its own privacy terms. This is the only reason the app has internet permission.
  • The app never records or saves audio.

Content

  • All facts, questions and stories were checked for accuracy, safety and suitability for ages 8 to 14. Stories model safe choices: rivers, thin ice, electrics and hot water always involve a grown-up or a safety line.
  • A teacher-friendly review page lists every item, so the school can read everything a child will see.

The grown-ups area

  • Opening it takes a times-table question written in words. This stops younger children by accident. It is not a password.
  • It holds the settings, the progress summary and "Delete all progress".

Honest notes

  • The app has not yet had a formal privacy or legal review. We don't claim it complies with any particular law.
  • Some phones may copy app data when moving to a new phone. The app asks Android not to, but not every phone maker allows that.
  • Buddy's voice uses the phone's own text-to-speech. A phone owner can set that to use online voices in the phone's settings. The app doesn't ask for this.

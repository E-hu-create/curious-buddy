Curious Buddy

A safe, offline science companion for children aged 8 to 14.

Children explore science with Buddy, a friendly teal creature with a golden backpack. They solve detective cases, choose what happens in stories, play science games and ask Buddy questions, all in a voice-first app that reads everything aloud.

Fits your curriculum

  • Supports 25 of the 28 UK primary science units, 13 of them strongly.
  • Matched to more than 60 Cambridge Primary Science objectives and more than 30 US science standards (NGSS). See the attached curriculum map.
  • Teaches thinking skills too: using evidence, fair tests and spotting patterns.

Safe by design

  • Works fully offline. No accounts, ads, chat or tracking.
  • Every answer is written in advance and reviewed. It is not an AI chatbot.
  • Worried or unsafe children are pointed kindly to a trusted adult.
  • See the attached safety and privacy statement.

Made for real classrooms

  • No internet or logins, so it works anywhere, on one shared device or many.
  • Reads aloud, uses large buttons, works with the phone's larger text setting and has a calmer-motion option, so it helps children who are still building reading confidence.
  • Content from many places, including Ghana and wider Africa, such as Ananse, Kente, Mansa Musa and Wangari Maathai.
  • Every fact can be checked by teachers on one printable review page.

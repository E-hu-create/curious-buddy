Curious Buddy: phone checklist

Try each one on the phone. Tick it if it works. If something looks wrong, write the number and what happened, then tell Claude, for example "7: the robot didn't move". About 45 to 60 minutes in all. You don't have to do it in one go.

Before you start

  • Open the newest Curious Buddy on the phone.
  • Turn the sound up, so you can hear Buddy and the sound effects.
  • For each one, check: it opens, it looks right, Buddy speaks, you can finish it, and nothing freezes or looks cut off.

Home and Buddy

Look at the app icon on the phone's home screen. You should see: a clear, bold Buddy icon that stands out.

On Home, type "Why is the sky blue?" and tap Ask. Then swipe the suggestions sideways and tap one. You should see: a small box, a short answer that Buddy reads aloud, and suggestions in one line.

Tap the microphone, say "Tell me a joke", check the words, then tap Ask. You should see: Buddy asks first before using the microphone, then shows what you said. If the phone can't listen offline, Buddy says so kindly.

Play any game and get one right and one wrong. You should hear: short, gentle sounds that aren't too loud or harsh.

Game Zone

Open Game Zone. You should see: a big tile for every game, each with a picture and a yellow button, and How to Play instructions.

Play Memory Match, Pattern Power and Spot the Change, one round each.

Guide Buddy to the chest and collect gems.

Plan some steps, press Go, and watch the robot follow them.

Unscramble a word from its clue.

Listen and pick what made the sound. Check: each sound is clear and matches its answer.

Play a whole game against Buddy.

Pop the bubbles that make the target number.

Put things into the right groups. Tap a wrong one too, to see what happens.

Turn the wires until the bulb lights up.

Fill the grid so no picture repeats.

Tap the planets into the right order.

Learning

Open 2 or 3 of the 10 topics and answer a few questions. You should see: an icon for each topic, stars for right answers, and an explanation after each question.

Play at least 2 of the newer cases, for example The Puddle That Vanished and The Case of the Shrinking Shadow, all the way to "Case solved".

Play at least 2 of the newer stories, for example The Whale Who Lost Her Song, to an ending. Check: each picture matches the scene.

Buddy and grown-ups

Open the Wardrobe and put something on Buddy. You should hear: "My … looks great!", and you see Buddy wearing it.

Scroll to the bottom of Home and tap Grown-ups. Answer the times-table check. You should see: Progress at a glance, the settings, and the jump links working.

In Grown-ups, turn Buddy's voice off. Close the app completely, then open it again. You should see: the voice is still off. Turn it back on.

When you're done

Tell Claude which numbers worked, and what went wrong with any others. A message like this is perfect: "All fine except 10 and 14. In 10 the frog sound is too quiet, and in 14 the bulb didn't light."
